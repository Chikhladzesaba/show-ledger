# Show Ledger

**Revenue tracking for YouTubers and digital creators.** See what you earn, what you spend, and whether the work pays off, by month, quarter and year.

**Live app:** chikhladzesaba.github.io/show-ledger

Your numbers stay in your own browser. No account, no server, nothing is sent anywhere.

---

## What it does

### Log money in and money out
- **Income streams built for creators:** Platform revenue (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Twitch), Sponsorships, Fan support, Affiliate, Products, plus your own.
- **Cost categories:** People, Production, Equipment, Software & tools, Promotion, plus your own.
- **Sub-types** for every stream and category, and a **Brand** field for sponsorships that remembers past brands.
- Entries for **a month, a quarter or a year**. Longer ones are spread evenly across their months.

### Real-life money, not just a list
- **Paid or Expected:** totals count money that actually arrived. Money still owed to you and bills still to pay are shown separately, with overdue ones highlighted.
- **Recurring entries:** a monthly deal or fee adds itself each month, ready to confirm with one tap.
- **Barter:** products or services instead of money, with an estimated value (market price or your usual rate). Kept separate from cash, with a "total value" view.
- **Team hours:** log a person's hours and fixed or hourly pay. See cost per hour and how hours change month to month.
- **USD and GEL:** each entry keeps the exchange rate it was paid at. Reports in the main currency you choose.
- **Several channels or shows** with an "All channels" view.

### See how you're doing
- **Overview:** income, costs, profit, return per 1 spent, compared with the previous period.
- **Monthly chart** of income vs costs, and breakdowns by stream and category.
- **Tax reserve:** set a percentage and see how much to keep aside.
- **Next month forecast** from recurring and expected entries.
- **Summary** of any period: print it, save it as PDF, or copy it as text.

### Built for everyday use
- Repeating entries are grouped into one row that opens like a dropdown, with **Edit all** and **Mark all paid**.
- Works on phones: the chart fits the screen and entries show as cards.

## Privacy

All data is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. Nobody else can see it, including the person who built the app.

That also means: **clearing your browser data, or switching to another browser or device, starts the app empty.**

---

## How it's built

- Plain **HTML, CSS and JavaScript**: no frameworks, no build step.
- Data saved with the browser's **localStorage**.
- Hosted on **GitHub Pages**.

Files:
- `index.html`: the page layout and styles
- `app.js`: the app's behavior and calculations
